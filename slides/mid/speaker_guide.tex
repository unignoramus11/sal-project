\sect{Be careful to say}
\small
\begin{itemize}
\item The 50-token manual audit has \textbf{not} been done; say ``it is the next check''. Do not present the drafted TextGrids as hand annotation.
\item RQ3 is \textbf{one seed}: distance improves, Recall@1 does not.
\item On PRiSM's release, speakers are \textbf{estimated} from voice clusters (no speaker IDs); the 98\% overlap is measured on our rebuild.
\item The order gain of +19 is on PRiSM's release; on our rebuild it is about +4 with shared speakers, and zero or negative on new speakers.
\end{itemize}
